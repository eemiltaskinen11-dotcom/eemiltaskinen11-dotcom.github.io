Ferrari
Ferrari facts
Ferrari is an iconic Italian luxury sports car manufacturer and racing team based in Maranello, Italy. Founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1939, the company built its first car in 1947 and has since become a global symbol of speed, luxury, and wealth.Key Facts About FerrariFounding and Heritage: Enzo Ferrari initially started the company as Scuderia Ferrari in 1929 to sponsor amateur drivers, before moving into manufacturing road cars to fund his racing passion.The Logo: The famous logo features a Prancing Horse (Cavallino Rampante) on a yellow background, honoring Italian fighter pilot Francesco Baracca.Motorsport Dominance: Ferrari is the oldest and most successful team in Formula 1 history, holding the record for the most Constructors' and Drivers' Championships.Iconic Models: Over the decades, Ferrari has produced legendary supercars like the F40, Enzo, LaFerrari, and modern models like the SF90 Stradale.The Signature Color: While Ferraris come in many colors today, the traditional racing color is Rosso Corsa (Racing Red).
